THE STORY

Hermeneia grew from a decade of experience working inside healthcare operations—where some of the most consequential problems are often hidden in ordinary workflows.

Across medical records, clinical research, patient access, and healthcare technology, the same pattern kept appearing: operational teams generate enormous amounts of information, yet the systems used to manage that work don't always make the underlying process visible.

Hermeneia was created to explore that gap through independent projects using operational analysis, data, and technology to understand how healthcare workflows function, identify where friction occurs, and make complex processes easier to see.

Across each project, the goal is to connect operations, systems, and data so the full picture emerges.
