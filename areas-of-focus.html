HEALTHCARE OPERATIONS
ARE SYSTEMS.

Healthcare operations rarely break in isolation. A delay in one part of a workflow can affect the data, documentation, handoffs, and decisions that follow.

Hermeneia examines these relationships across workflows and operational data to understand where friction occurs, what the data reveals, and where greater visibility can support better decisions.
